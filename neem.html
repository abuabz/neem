Our story

A touch by nature

Neem is a premium face tissue brand from Kerala, India. Our name and our leaf mark come from the neem tree, which has long been part of everyday life here.

We set out to make a tissue that feels soft, looks premium and fits easily into daily routines, without a premium price tag.

Our promise

Softness that cares, nature that protects. Every bottle is made to feel good in your hands and on your skin.
